## Personalization Checklist

Before sending ANY outreach, ensure:

- [ ] Spelled recipient's name correctly
- [ ] Researched company and recent news
- [ ] Referenced specific pain point or achievement
- [ ] Customized case study or example
- [ ] Adjusted industry/context references
- [ ] Verified email address is current
- [ ] Personalized CTA based on research
- [ ] Used appropriate tone for their role

## Follow-Up Sequence

### If No Response:

**Day 1**: Initial email
↓
**Day 5**: First follow-up ("Just wanted to make sure this didn't get lost...")
↓
**Day 10**: Second follow-up (Reference something new, different angle)
↓
**Day 14**: Final follow-up (Short, direct, option to connect on LinkedIn)
↓
**Day 21**: Archive and revisit in 3-6 months

## Response Rate Benchmarks to Target

- **Email Open Rate**: 25-35%
- **Click-Through Rate**: 2-5%
- **Response Rate**: 2-8%
- **Meeting Rate**: 0.5-2% of outreach

**Goal**: For every 100 outreach emails, aim for 2-5 positive responses or meetings.

---

## Converting First Meetings

When you get a meeting:

1. **Preparation** (30 min before)
   - Review their company
   - Identify 2-3 specific pain points
   - Prepare relevant case study
   - Have diagnostic framework ready

2. **During Call** (15-30 min)
   - Ask questions more than pitch
   - Listen for pain points
   - Share relevant example
   - Outline next steps clearly

3. **Follow-Up** (within 24 hours)
   - Send agenda/notes from call
   - Include relevant case study
   - Propose specific next step
   - Include timeline

## Email Signature Best Practices

✅ **DO Include:**
- Full name
- Title/Role
- Company name
- Email address
- Phone number (optional)
- LinkedIn profile link
- Website/company link

❌ **DON'T Include:**
- Lengthy disclaimers
- Multiple images/logos
- Social media links you don't maintain
- Personal quotes/mottos
- "Sent from my iPhone" (remove this!)

## Tips for High-Converting Outreach

1. **Be Specific** - Mention exact company, role, or challenge
2. **Lead with Value** - Show benefit, not features
3. **Keep It Short** - 3-4 sentences max for initial email
4. **Make It Easy** - Include specific time options for meetings
5. **Use Social Proof** - Reference similar clients or results
6. **Create Urgency** - Limited time slots, new capabilities, etc.
7. **Be Authentic** - Write like you talk, not like a robot
8. **Follow Up** - Most deals close in follow-ups 3+
9. **Personalize Everything** - Templates are starting points only
10. **Track Results** - Monitor what works and iterate
